## 📧 Email Automation Sequence

### **5-Email Welcome Sequence**

**Email 1: Immediate Reply (0 minutes)**
- Subject: "Thank You! We Got Your Message"
- Body: Thank them, set expectations, link to booking calendar

**Email 2: Value Add (24 hours later)**
- Subject: "Here's How We Help [Industry] Like You"
- Body: Case study or testimonial, free resource

**Email 3: Social Proof (48 hours later)**
- Subject: "3 Reasons Why [Industry] Choose Us"
- Body: Benefits, more social proof

**Email 4: FAQ (72 hours later)**
- Subject: "Common Questions (Answered)"
- Body: Address objections, answer FAQ

**Email 5: Soft CTA (96 hours later)**
- Subject: "Let's Chat About Your [Goal]"
- Body: Call to action to book call or meeting
